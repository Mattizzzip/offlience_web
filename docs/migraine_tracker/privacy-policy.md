# Privacy Policy

**Migraine, Aura & Pill Tracker**  
Effective date: 4 September 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how Individual Entrepreneur Andrei Osipau (“we”, “us”) handles information in the mobile application **Migraine, Aura & Pill Tracker** (also shown in the app as Migraine Control) for iOS and Android.

The app is a personal self-tracking tool. It does not diagnose, prescribe treatment, or replace medical advice.

## 1. We do not collect your data

We do not operate an account system, cloud backend, analytics service, crash reporter, or advertising network for this app.

We do not transmit your personal or health information from the device to us or to our partners in a way that would let us access it later. In Apple’s App Store Connect terms, this means **we do not collect data from this app**.

Opening this Privacy Policy or the Terms of Use from the app launches your device’s browser and visits [offlience.com](https://offlience.com). That website visit is not a transfer of your diary, profile, or medication data.

## 2. Information stored only on your device

You may enter information so the app can work on your phone or tablet. That information stays in local storage on the device (SQLite and related local files). We cannot see it.

Depending on how you use the app, this may include:

**Profile**

- First name and last name  
- Date of birth  
- Optional profile photo (copied onto the device)

**Health diary (entered by you)**

- Calendar marks: migraine, aura, headache, medication taken  
- Aura types, duration, and notes  
- Pain intensity  
- Triggers (for example stress, sleep, alcohol, weather, menstruation, or a note you type)  
- Medication names, doses, and schedules  
- Daily medication records

**App settings**

- Language preference  
- Onboarding and disclaimer acknowledgements  
- Reminder settings

This information is used only to show the calendar, analytics, reminders, and reports **on your device**.

## 3. Permissions

The app may ask the system for:

| Permission | Why |
|---|---|
| Notifications | Medication reminders at times you set |
| Photo library | Choosing a profile photo |

The iOS binary includes a file-picker library used to import a backup (ZIP or JSON). That library links camera and location APIs, so the App Store requires purpose strings in Info.plist. **The app does not use your location and does not track you.** Profile photos are chosen with the system photo picker, not by uploading images to us.

You can deny permissions. The diary still works; reminders or a profile photo may be unavailable.

## 4. Sharing, export, and import

You can export a PDF report, CSV, or ZIP backup and share it through the system share sheet (for example Files, Mail, or another app you choose).

That sharing is **your action**. We do not receive the file. Anyone you send it to will see the contents you exported (including profile and health data). Treat backups as sensitive.

You can import a backup you previously exported. Import replaces local data on that device.

We do not sell or share your information with advertisers, data brokers, or other third parties.

## 5. Tracking and advertising

The app does not:

- track you across other companies’ apps or websites  
- use the Advertising Identifier (IDFA)  
- show ads  
- use App Tracking Transparency  
- include analytics or marketing SDKs  

## 6. Children

The app is not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect information from children. Date of birth is stored only on the device if you enter it.

## 7. Security and deletion

Data remains on the device you use. Protect the device with a passcode, Face ID, or another lock. Uninstalling the app, or clearing the app’s data in system settings, deletes local data.

We cannot recover data from a lost device or an uninstalled app. Keep a backup if you need a copy.

## 8. Changes

If this policy changes, we will update the effective date and the version published at [https://offlience.com/migraine_tracker/privacy_policy](https://offlience.com/migraine_tracker/privacy_policy). Material changes will be reflected in the app store listing or in the app when required.
